## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, forward-looking aesthetic tailored for postgraduate research in artificial intelligence. It bridges high-rigor academia with cutting-edge computational technology, balancing the historic gravitas of Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos (UNMSM) with the crisp velocity of modern AI development.

The design movement combines **Modern High-Tech Corporate** with **Micro-Glassmorphic Precision**:
- **Pristine structural clarity:** Ample whitespace, high-contrast legibility, and architectural typography instill academic credibility.
- **Electric computational energy:** Vibrant neural gradients running from electric blue to violet, counterpointed by cyan node indicators and reserved institutional maroon marks.
- **Calculated polish:** Subtle hairline dividers, delicate borders, and low-opacity ambient backdrops convey the feel of modern developer environments, research documentation, and real-time inference dashboards.

The emotional goal is to inspire intellectual ambition, clarity of thought, and technological mastery among researchers, enterprise partners, and postgraduate candidates.

## Colors

The palette balances clean cool-gray backdrops with high-potency spectral accents:

- **Primary (`#3B82F6`) & Secondary (`#6366F1`):** Electric blue and indigo serve as the core anchors for primary interactive components, primary CTA buttons, neural graph connectors, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6`):** Violet acts as an accent gradient end-point and visual tag for advanced machine learning concepts, papers, or special research categories.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate-navy replaces generic black for maximum contrast without harshness, providing exceptional typography crispness across 400 to 700 weights.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `cyan-highlight` (`#06B6D4`): Employed sparingly for status beacons, active telemetry pills, metric callouts, and hover micro-glows.
  - `unmsm-maroon` (`#881337`): Institutional San Marcos heritage accent reserved for academic seals, institutional accreditation badges, thesis identifiers, and milestone insignias.
  - `surface-base` (`#FFFFFF`), `surface-subtle` (`#F8FAFC`), and `surface-muted` (`#F1F5F9`): Alternating section rhythms that segregate dense curriculum modules, publication rosters, and dataset portals without heavy borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a rigorous technical dialogue:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Infuses computational structure and geometric personality. Tight negative tracking gives headlines a sleek, modern algorithmic character, especially in section titles, stats, and thesis topics.
- **Body & Labels (`Inter`):** Delivers clean readability for prolonged reading sessions (curriculum plans, research abstracts, formulas, and tabular data) across dense viewports.
- **Special Code/Data treatment:** Mathematical notations, model weights, and hyperparameter metrics leverage `Space Grotesk` at smaller optical scales with calibrated tracking to maintain technical presence without requiring an over-stylized monospace.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes a structured **12-column responsive fluid grid** with strict rhythmic section alternations:

- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) page margin, max-width constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) page margin.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margin.
- **Section Banding:** Sections transition cleanly between `#FFFFFF` and alternating bands of `#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9` to organically group modules (e.g., Hero -> Research Lines -> Faculty -> Publications) without visual clutter.
- **Component Flow:** Internal card padding is standardized to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate boundary definition and luminous ambient lighting rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or cool tinted `#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9`.
  - **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` paired with hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  - **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):** Elevates via a combined physical lift and soft blue-violet diffusion: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.06)`, with border transitioning to `#CBD5E1` or `#93C5FD`.
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Navbars):** Translucent backdrop-blur glass effect (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision through **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape rules:

- **Base Radii:** Standard cards, panels, inputs, and modular code surfaces use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Nested & Large Containers:** Larger dashboard cards, modal views, and preview surfaces use `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px).
- **Pill Elements:** Interactive chips, filter pills, AI model status indicators, and category tags utilize full rounded pill styling (`9999px`) to contrast against sharp architectural card grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #3B82F6 0%, #6366F1 100%)`) with white text, font `label-lg`, smooth hover transition that deepens saturation and applies an ambient electric blue drop shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, deep slate text (`#1E293B`). On hover, border shifts to `#3B82F6` with subtle tinted background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Institutional / Badge Action:** Subtle maroon tint (`#881337`) with 10% opacity fill and crisp `#881337` text for official UNMSM certification downloads and institutional guidelines.

### Cards & Modules
- Clean, mathematical aesthetic. Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Interactive research cards incorporate a subtle `2px` top accent border or corner indicator that reveals an electric-to-violet gradient upon hover, coupled with a smooth `translateY(-2px)` transition.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
- Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, internal padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state: Ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.2)` with border color shifting cleanly to `#3B82F6`. Placeholder text rendered in `#94A3B8`.

### Chips & Badges
- **AI Status / Topic Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `label-sm`.
  - Machine Learning & Deep Learning: `#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, `#DBEAFE` border.
  - Generative AI & LLMs: `#F5F3FF` background, `#7C3AED` text, `#EDE9FE` border.
  - Active Node / Telemetry: Cyan highlight pill with `#ECFEFF` background, `#0891B2` text, accompanied by a pulsing `6px` live dot.
  - UNMSM Heritage: `#FFF1F2` background, `#881337` text, `#FFE4E6` border.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Base state: Bordered in `#CBD5E1` over white ground with `0.25rem` radius (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Active state: Filled with `#3B82F6` displaying crisp white vector iconography, accompanied by a subtle focus ring.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Academic Paper Tile:** Features publication metadata (authors, conference, DOI), accompanied by a Space Grotesk citation count pill and an inline PDF action link.
- **Cohort Milestone Tracker:** Linear horizontal node chart featuring vibrant connected lines between semantic checkpoints with cyan status markers.
